# SiFi-GAN: synthetic device feasibility, not a trained daegeum

The preceding pretrained BigVGAN039/040 audition was rejected for daegeum
identity and unstable vibrato. This directory checks a different architecture
before any instrument training or further listening output is justified.

`probe_generator_mps.py` imports the complete, unmodified official
[SiFi-GAN](https://github.com/chomeyama/SiFiGAN) generator at commit
`cdb20daeb4da4a126077819091706ee90522c7a8`, using its full512-channel/43-feature
configuration. Source is MIT; upstream LICENSE is retained in the private
checkout. **No public pretrained weights are downloaded or licensed by this
code-license check.** Public voice checkpoints are not daegeum instruments.

The sole32-frame synthetic batch has43 random conditioning channels and an
artificial220Hz pitch curve, with±12cent/5Hz modulation. Excitation phase is
generated by the official CPU float64 `SignalGenerator`, then explicitly
converted to float32 for MPS, following the training Collater's CPU-source
ordering. Dilation factors use the official24kHz formula and dense factors
`[0.5,1,4,8]`, with stage lengths160/640/1920/3840. No source waveform or
dataset labels are read. Both generator outputs enter a diagnostic squared
loss, and gradients are checked. There is **no optimizer update**.

The official training CLI selects CUDA orCPU, notMPS. This is a separate
operator probe, not an assertion that the upstream CLI runs on MPS unchanged.
There is no CPU fallback or reduced-channel retry. A watchdog caps the process
at300seconds and8GiB conservative RSS+MPS-driver allocation, retaining at
least12GiB free disk. Only a private0600JSON report is written; no audio or
checkpoint is saved. It records exact source/probe hashes, device, parameter
count, shapes, gradient presence, timing and resource observations. Generator
downsample layers constructed but unused by the official forward can
legitimately have no gradient and are reported by name.

```text
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 PYTORCH_ENABLE_MPS_FALLBACK=0 \
  python -B probe_generator_mps.py --upstream PINNED_PRIVATE_CHECKOUT \
  --output NEW_PRIVATE_OUTPUT_DIRECTORY
```

Use the already prepared private Python3.12/Torch2.10 environment, with its
read-only dependencies; the wrapper installs or downloads nothing. The new
private dependency target adds h5py3.16.0(BSD-3-Clause) and numpy2.3.5. Importing
the upstream package needs h5py even though this probe reads no HDF5 file.

Even success would establish **only one full generator forward/backward on a
tiny synthetic batch**, not viable instrument training or vibrato quality.
Still unverified: native-original24kHz feature extraction/normalization with
daegeum's full pitch range, recording-disjoint fit/holdout data, discriminator
and official mel/residual/adversarial losses, optimizer state, checkpoint
resume, sustained resource usage, and held-out original-vs-reconstruction
listening. A score/expression-to-acoustic-feature model remains a separate
task. Do not route new scores or game audio through random generator weights.

Original recordings and game defaults are untouched. No new listening files
are generated. If recordings are used in later authorized work, retain:
**국악기 음원 제공 — 국립국악원 (공공누리 제1유형)**.

## Actual2026-10-23 result

The single full-generator MPS probe completed in26.55seconds including imports;
synchronized forward/backward took13.89seconds. All11,303,652parameters were
onMPS, with CPU fallback explicitly disabled. Both outputs were finite
`[1,1,3840]`;282parameter tensors had finite nonzero gradients, none had a
present-but-zero gradient. The six absent gradients were exactly the unused
final sn/fn downsample bias/weight_g/weight_v tensors described above. No
optimizer step, real feature extraction or acoustic reconstruction occurred.
The final observation was262,340,608bytes RSS and272,973,824bytes MPS-driver
allocation; these are **end-of-run observations, not peak memory**. Upstream
tracked-file hashes were unchanged. The private code/dependencies/report folder
occupies about53MiB. Seven lightweight boundary tests and an independent code
review do not extend this result to actual training or sound quality.

Next gate, not yet passed: connect real native-original24kHz features and test
the official discriminator, losses, optimizer and checkpoint resume in a
bounded run, retaining recording-disjoint evaluation. Only after that can a
timed daegeum training pilot be justified. Public voice weights and the
rejected pretrained BigVGAN decoder remain excluded from that path.

## 2026-10-24: actual recordings, official GAN losses and disk-state restore

`prepare_real_features.py` prepares a deliberately small **execution gate**,
not the final training corpus. It verifies original-file hashes from the
existing public NGC manifest, decodes native48kHz sus04/vib39 and native44.1kHz
Arirang002 directly to24kHz (never upsampling old16kHz copies), and preserves
one4.8s excerpt per file. sus04/vib39 are fit; Arirang002 is a source-file
holdout which was used in earlier research and is **not blind**. No score or
performance-generalization claim follows from this split.

The43raw channels are40mel-cepstral coefficients (`pysptk.sp2mc`, order39,
alpha0.466) and3coded WORLD aperiodicity bands. Harvest uses70..1800Hz/5ms;
CheapTrick and D4C useFFT2048, D4C threshold0.85. Raw F0 is retained separately
from interpolated/20Hz-low-pass continuousF0. The final WORLD endpoint alone
is discarded to align960frames with115,200samples. Unlike the upstream
extractor's defaults, this adapter uses no causal70Hz high-pass (keeping raw
target/features aligned), FFT2048 rather than1024, and explicit float64 rather
than removed `np.float`. Fit-only per-channel population mean/std never uses
the holdout. Constant-channel scales map to1, not a learned heldout statistic.

Actual preparation took5.55seconds. Voicing estimates were787/960(sus04),
960/960(vib39),909/960(Arirang002). **The two fit excerpts cover only about
253..271Hz.** Holdout F0 estimates reach1222Hz, and16.28%of its voiced frames
have all-bin D4C aperiodicity>=0.999. These are estimator warnings, not proof
of musical correctness; the fit data are plainly insufficient for a full
instrument. High-register analysis and wider training coverage remain open.

`train_real_gate.py` uses the full official SiFi-GAN generator and default
UnivNet discriminator (3resolution+5period heads), unmodified upstream code,
and batch1×70frames versus the published batch16. All G/D/loss computation is
MPS float32 with CPU fallback disabled; only excitation phase starts onCPU.
The residual F0 range is explicitly70..1800Hz rather than clamping at840Hz.
The gate starts with adversarial-active step1, not the upstream step0 warmup:

- G: `45*mel + residual + 2*feature_matching + adversarial`.
- D: summed real/fake LSGAN losses across all8heads.
- Both Adam optimizers: lr2e-4, betas(0.5,0.9), gradient clipping10; official
  MultiStepLR settings retained. After each G update a fresh G output feeds D.

The actual run completed in24.13seconds: G11,303,652parameters and
D41,381,552parameters, each updated twice with finite gradients/losses.
Step1/vib39 took14.16seconds and the warmed step2/sus04 took0.805seconds;
two different crops are **not a convergence comparison or a reliable
long-run speed estimate**. The first-step611,753,444-byte private checkpoint
includes both models, both Adam states, schedulers and CPU/MPS RNG. It was
loaded with `weights_only=True` and a scoped stdlibCounter allowlist, preserving
OrderedDict metadata/Counter types. A deliberately perturbed G parameter was
restored; the complete typed numeric-state digest matched and both Adam
counters advanced to2after the next step. This is an **in-process disk restore
and continuation**, not a fresh-process restart/crash-recovery test.

Final observations were563,789,824bytes RSS and2,666,283,008bytes MPS driver
allocation (not peaks). The new private folder occupies about591MiB, below the
announced revised1GB limit; initial500MB was raised before writing the full
resume state. The600second/8GiB conservative memory/12GiB disk-floor guard
was retained. No generated audio is saved or presented; two updates do not
train acceptable daegeum timbre or vibrato. Models, original/derived data,
checkpoint and reports remain outsideGit. Twenty-four SiFi-GAN boundary tests
(including a tiny CPU state roundtrip, no models) pass.

Use prepared private dependencies: existing Python3.12/Torch2.10/librosa,
PyWORLD0.3.5 from its read-only environment, and a new separate target for
[pysptk1.0.1](https://pypi.org/project/pysptk/)(MIT; installed LICENSE retained).
No existing environment is modified and no public voice weights are used.

```text
python -B prepare_real_features.py --manifest PRIVATE_NGC_SOURCE_MANIFEST \

PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 PYTORCH_ENABLE_MPS_FALLBACK=0 \
```

Next is wider pitch/articulation/phrase coverage with independent heldout

bounded training pilot and unaltered-pitch heldout reconstruction. Only
listening can accept daegeum identity; score-to-feature performance generation
is still a separate stage. No new game BGM is ready.

## 2026-10-25: broader corpus and bounded scratch training pilot

`prepare_corpus.py` reads13lossless native originals in full: sustained,
vibrato, deep-vibrato scales, Taepyeongga/daruchigi, noniro/nira/staccato/
crescendo, and4Arirang phrases. Their archive ledger labels are retained;
no new articulation labels are inferred from sound. ALL Arirang phrases are
one evaluation-only title group, together with a separate deep-vibrato source.
They were historically inspected and are not blind. Eight native files are
48kHz and five44.1kHz; no MP3 or old16k feature input is used.

Actual preparation took336.50seconds, preserving975.155seconds from
975.179043seconds of originals (24.043ms total sub5ms tails only). Thirteen
NPZs contain raw24k waveform, WORLD F0/cf0/mcep+bap,64ms centered24kHz FFT-YIN
and RMS, and the unmodified all-bin-D4C-AP>=0.999 flag. The independent YIN
uses a5ms grid and first CMNDF<0.17 trough;1-CMNDF is an uncalibrated support
proxy, not a truth label. Source/decoded-PCM duplicates and original changes
are rejected. Original waveforms/features are never repaired by the gate.

`audit_corpus.py` checks70-frame/350ms windows every35frames. Eligibility
requires80%source-YIN support>=0.90,95%Harvest agreement within50c on that
source-fixed support, and less than10%all-bin-AP-one among Harvest-voiced
frames. There is NO pitch-derivative or vibrato-depth restriction. These
provisional criteria underrepresent legitimate breath, attack and staccato;
rejected windows are not declared bad performances. All1,148heldout candidate
windows, including failures, remain visible in the gate.

The actual fit gate accepted2,097of4,404overlapping windows, whose **unique
frame union is395.5seconds of772.84seconds raw fit audio**, not733.95seconds
of unique data. Fit-only pitch-bin counts are476/890/535/196in200–400,
400–800,800–1200,1200–1800Hz; no70–200Hz window passed. Staccato contributes
only2windows/0.7unique seconds, daruchigi6windows/2.1seconds. This is not
comprehensive articulation coverage. Independent union-frame recomputation
matched the normalizer to1.2e-13, with zero heldout contribution.

AP rejection counts need care: of1,379fit windows with that reason,1,292have
no Harvest-voiced frame (undefined fraction), versus87with definedAP>=10%.
Only46high-reference-bin fit windows have that reason. Multiple rejection
reasons overlap; do not call all AP rejections proven D4C errors. A separate
amplitude-domain limitation remains:30,313of9,492,000accepted waveform samples
(0.319%) exceed|1|, maximum1.38687. The official Tanh output cannot reproduce
those peaks at unchanged gain. This pilot preserves unit gain; it neither
clips targets nor applies per-note normalization. This fact is not evidence
that peak handling caused earlier timbral failures.

`train_corpus_pilot.py` starts full G/defaultUnivNet D from scratch with the
new audited fit-only normalizer, not the earlier two-step checkpoint. Sampling
balances(case,pitch-bin)groups and chooses random audited windows within them.
Only bound corpus rows marked fit+accepted can reach the optimizer; protected
Arirang/deep-vibrato identities and duplicate/split-leaking hashes are checked
again. There is no automatic CPU fallback, reduced channel count, feature
flattening, source-waveform insertion or output filtering.

The maximum is500updates,20minutes/8GiB conservative memory/12GiB free disk;
training soft-stops at1,050seconds to reserve final evaluation/save time. Total
announced additional storage is2GB. One full resume checkpoint and one small
G-only checkpoint are saved privately, including fit normalizer and data-gate
identity. The full state retains both optimizers/schedulers, CPU/MPS and NumPy
sampler RNG, current group permutation, next position and cycle-boundary rule.

Fixed evaluation uses27fit group representatives and3windows per heldout
recording (15total), NOT all1,148heldout candidates. Two exact4.8-second clips
(deep-vibrato and Arirang001) are reconstructed before/after with the same
excitation seed and no pitch shift. Evaluation preserves training RNG/mode and
never selects weights or adjusts hyperparameters. `pilot_metrics.py` keeps
source-only pitch denominators, reports dropouts/octave errors and unchanged-
time vibrato/level proxies without declaring naturalness. Conditional metrics
on successfully measured output never hide lost reference frames. Saved NPZs
are internal diagnostics, not approved auditions or new music.

Code boundaries are covered by42new synthetic tests (6preparation,12audit,
10metrics,14pilot) plus24earlier readiness tests. No new dependencies or public
weights are downloaded. Original NGC credit remains required. Successful short
training would not establish convergence, daegeum identity, stable vibrato or
score-to-performance generation; only matched listening can approve sound.
